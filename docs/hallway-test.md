# Five-person hallway test (usability phase 4, item 14)

Five writers who have never seen StoryTool. One session each, about 30 minutes. Watch; do not help.
The first-run measures on the admin Feedback page (First run) show the same flows in numbers.

## Before each session

- A fresh Google account or a new StoryTool sign-up; no stories yet.
- Desktop Chrome, window at full size; screen recording on, with their consent.
- Say once: "I'm testing the app, not you. Think out loud. I can't answer questions until the end."
- Hand over one card at a time. Start a timer per card.

## Task cards

**Card 1: Write a scene (stop at 10 minutes)**
> You have an idea for a story. Start one in StoryTool and write its opening few lines.

**Card 2: Add a character (stop at 8 minutes)**
> Your story has a main character. Add them to the story, with one thing they want.

**Card 3: Find a continuity problem (stop at 10 minutes)**
> Open the example story "The Last Lantern" from the guided tour (storytool.onrender.com/try).
> Somewhere a detail contradicts itself. Use StoryTool to find one problem.

## What to note (one row per card)

| Card | Finished? | Time | First thing they clicked | Where they hesitated (> 5 s) | What they said | Wrong turns |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Watch especially:
- Card 1: which start card they choose, whether they find Write or start in Design, whether they notice the "Your first hour" checklist or the first-use tip.
- Card 2: whether they look in Design → Characters, the side panel's Cast tab, or the / menu.
- Card 3: whether they find Check my story, and whether "Needs attention" makes sense to them.

## After all five cards (5 minutes)

1. What was the most confusing moment?
2. What did you expect to find that wasn't there?
3. Would you use this for a real book? Why or why not?

## Afterwards

- List every stumble seen by two or more people. Rank by how many people hit it, then how long it cost.
- Fix the top three (the roadmap item: "fix where they get stuck"). Record each as a QA item with
  the card, what happened and the fix.
- Compare with First run on the Feedback page a week later: time to first scene, checklist steps,
  tools tried and dropped.
